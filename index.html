Money rules

Risk 1 to 2% of your balance per trade

Cost of a losing trade = stop distance × UGX per 1.00 move. At 0.01 lot on USOIL that was about 41,600 UGX per 1.00 (an estimate from your own trade; confirm it in MT5).

    Two markets, one plan

    • Same 6 steps for USOIL and UKOIL. Mark floors on each chart separately. Brent and WTI prices differ.
    • They usually move together. Trade one at a time.
    • Check UKOIL’s cost per 1.00 move in MT5 before sizing.

    Good to know

    • Selling later: in a downtrend flip everything. Lower highs and lows, a ceiling instead of a floor.
