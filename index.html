Best time to trade

The best time is when the oil market is most active, because moves are cleaner and spreads are tighter. Times below are East Africa Time (EAT, your time), and I am assuming it is the zone you trade in.

Window (EAT)What happensFor FBM
About 10:00 to 15:00London is open and the US has not started, so trends formGood
About 15:00 to 19:00London and New York overlap, with the most volume and the biggest movesBest
About 19:30 to midnightLondon closes and volume thinsOkay, be careful
About midnight to 10:00Asian session, usually slow and range-boundWeakest, more false bounces

These times shift by about one hour when the US and UK change their clocks (UK on 25 Oct, US on 1 Nov).

Times to avoid

  • EIA inventory report: Wednesdays at 10:30 New York time, which is 17:30 EAT now and 18:30 after 1 Nov. Price jumps and spreads widen. Stay out from 20 minutes before until about 30 minutes after.
  • API report: Tuesdays around 23:30 EAT. It is a smaller version of the same effect.
  • Market open and close: The first 15 minutes after Sunday night's open and the last hour before the Friday close are choppy.
  • Daily break: Around midnight to 01:00 EAT, spreads widen.
  • OPEC+ meetings and big headlines: Check the news calendar each morning.

How to apply it

  • Pick one window of 2 to 3 hours. The 15:00 to 19:00 overlap is the strongest. Trade only then.
  • Mark your floors on H1 earlier in the day, before the window starts.
  • Keep your daily limit of 2 losing trades.
Your MT5 chart clock probably differs from your phone clock. MT5 shows the broker's server time, which is often 1 to 3 hours behind East Africa Time. Compare the time of the newest candle on your chart with your phone clock to find the gap. Then adjust the times above before using them.
My caution: These are general session patterns, not a tested edge for your plan. To find your own best window, record which hours your wins and losses came from over your next 20 trades.
